# KodiQuest Tanzania question-bank report

## Research record

Research was performed against official Tanzania Revenue Authority pages and the official TRA publication `Kodi na Ushuru Mbalimbali 2025/2026`. Review dates are recorded per source in `content/research-sources.js`. The primary review date was **2026-07-31**; the paying-taxes page was crawled and reviewed on **2026-07-30**.

## Results

- Total active verified questions: **105**.
- Level 1: **45** records: 30 core questions plus 15 revision questions.
- Level 2: **30** questions.
- Level 3: **30** questions.
- Short-answer / letter challenges: **68**.
- Multiple-choice challenges: **21**.
- Scenario challenges: **6**.
- Missing-word challenges: **2**.
- True/false challenges: **8**.
- Revision questions: 15.
- Every active question includes a verified official source, source URL, title, review date and verification status.

## Topic coverage

TRA identity and support, TIN and taxpayer registration, receipts and EFD, business records, individual income tax, tax payment and returns, VAT, PAYE, SDL, withholding tax, IDRAS, digital services, customs, imports, exports, compliance, declarations, assessments, Tax Clubs and taxpayer education.

## Excluded information

Exact rates, thresholds, deadlines, exemptions, penalties and procedural requirements were generally excluded from gameplay because they can change by law or financial year. A small number of source records are marked `requiresAnnualReview` when they are tied to the 2025/2026 publication.

No unresolved conflict was turned into an active question. Follow-up items are listed in `RESEARCH_REQUIRED.md`.

## Maintenance process

1. Re-open the official source.
2. Update `lastReviewed` and financial-year metadata.
3. Confirm the Kiswahili wording and English translation.
4. Validate answer construction, distractors and question type.
5. Run the question-bank validator and duplicate checks.
6. Remove or deactivate any question affected by a source change.
