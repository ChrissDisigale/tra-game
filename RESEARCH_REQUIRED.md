# Research follow-up

Official TRA pages and the TRA `Kodi na Ushuru Mbalimbali 2025/2026` publication were opened and reviewed on 2026-07-31. No active question was created from an unresolved conflict.

The following items require annual or procedural review before a production release:

- Rates, thresholds, payment dates, filing dates, exemptions, penalties and reliefs.
- Any change to IDRAS, Taxpayer Portal, EFD/VFD or digital-service workflows.
- Current contact details and TRA support channels.
- Current Tax Club programme information and school materials.
- Customs forms, import/export steps, tariff schedules and border procedures.
- Official wording and scope of any future tax education campaign.

The active prototype bank intentionally avoids most numerical questions and does not use unresolved details. Questions marked with a financial year carry `requiresAnnualReview: true` in their source metadata.
